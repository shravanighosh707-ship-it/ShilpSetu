# 🧵 ShilpSetu

## AI-Powered Platform for Marginalized Artisans

ShilpSetu is an AI-powered mobile application designed to help marginalized artisans showcase their products, simplify digital catalog creation, and connect directly with B2B buyers.

The platform aims to reduce dependence on intermediaries and periodic exhibitions by creating an accessible, year-round digital channel for business opportunities.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

**AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application for Marginalized Artisans**

### Challenges Addressed

- Limited access to consistent digital markets
- Dependence on intermediaries and physical exhibitions
- Difficulty creating professional product catalogs
- Language and digital-literacy barriers
- Challenges in reaching potential business buyers

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ShilpSetu aims to provide artisans with an easy-to-use digital platform that helps them:

- Create professional product listings with minimal effort
- Describe products using voice input
- Showcase their craftsmanship to potential buyers
- Improve their digital presence
- Explore business opportunities beyond physical exhibitions

The platform also focuses on helping B2B buyers discover artisans and their products.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧑‍🎨 Artisan-Side Features

- Artisan registration and login
- Artisan profile creation
- Product-detail input interface
- Product image upload
- AI-powered product catalog generation
- AI-assisted smart pricing

### 🛍️ Buyer-Side Features

- Buyer registration and login

### 🤖 AI-Powered Features

- Automated product-title and description generation
